## What the app does

Powder Run helps a coating shop record item intake, preparation, powder and lot information, oven loads, observed part-metal temperature and dwell, inspection, rework, disposition and job handoff. It can make local reports and backups. It is a recordkeeping tool, not a temperature sensor, automatic oven controller, laboratory test, regulatory certification, or proof by itself that a part has cured. Live Activity reminders and any estimated time are not evidence of cure. An operator remains responsible for checking the current powder manufacturer's technical data, measuring actual part-metal temperature, following safe shop practice, and deciding whether to release a part.

You are responsible for the accuracy of entries and for obtaining the rights needed to enter information about customers, workers or third parties. Shop-authored presets help with repeat setup but do not replace verification for a new lot, recipe, part or manufacturer instruction. Do not use the app as the sole safeguard for a hazardous operation.

The current app stores records locally. There is no developer-hosted account or sync between devices. You should keep your own backups and protect exported JSON, CSV and PDF files; when you share a file, your chosen destination controls that copy. An explicit restore replaces the current local ledger after validation. Device loss, removal of the app, a failed backup, or an incorrect restore can result in loss of local records.
